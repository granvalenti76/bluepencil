PROSE REVIEW.
Judge the prose like a demanding editor:
- does the text flow, or does the reader stumble?
- are the words chosen with care, or are they vague, repeated, imprecise?
- does each sentence follow from the last, or are sentences just placed side by side?
Be frank. If the prose is weak, say so, even when the text is correct: don't soften the judgment.
For each point to improve call "annota" with:
- citazione: the passage, copied exactly;
- problema: what doesn't work, in one sentence;
- proposta (optional): how you would rewrite it.
Write in English.
