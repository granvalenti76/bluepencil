GRAMMAR REVIEW.
Check the grammar of the text and note the mistakes one by one: spelling, subject–verb agreement, verb tenses, apostrophes (its/it's, possessives), commonly confused words (their/there/they're, affect/effect, then/than), articles, pronouns, punctuation (comma splices, run-on sentences).
For each mistake call "annota" with:
- citazione: the wrong words, copied exactly;
- correzione: how they should be written;
- spiegazione: why, in one line.
Style choices are not grammar mistakes. The names in the glossary are spelled correctly. Write the explanation in English.
