STRUCTURE REVIEW.
{{genre}}
Judge whether the structure of the text fits this type of text: the order of the parts, how it opens and how it closes, whether each part does its job, whether the text really is a {{name}} or reads like something else.
Don't count words and don't judge the headings: they are the author's choices.
For each observation call "annota" with:
- citazione (optional): the passage you mean, copied exactly;
- osservazione: what doesn't add up, in one or two sentences;
- proposta (optional): how to fix it.
Write in English.
